How it works

  • 1
    Pick a winner, every weekYour team must win outright — no draws or losses allowed. Only wins survive (unless a life line is used).
  • 2
    No repeats, all seasonOnce you pick a team, it's gone for good — pick United, that's probably your last pick for everyone.
  • 3
    Losing or drawing means you're outIf your chosen team loses or ties, you're out. You did NOT survive!
  • 4
    Missing the deadlineWe spin the wheel excluding the top 3 favorites. Miss 2 deadlines? You lose a life line + we spin. Miss 3? Another life line + we spin. Miss more than 3? We just spin.
  • 5
    Pick deadline30 minutes before the first kickoff of the gameweek, shown in your own local time.
  • 6
    Winner takes allLast survivor takes the full pot. Can split a maximum of 2 ways if exactly 2 players survive to the end.
  • 7
    Life line rule2 life lines per season. A draw with a life line survives but burns it. A win keeps it but locks it for 5 gameweeks.
